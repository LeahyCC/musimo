Musimo visualizer preview

The Dive study adapts the Sherwin Maxawow preset from butterchurn-presets
2.4.7 (MIT), by Flexi, martin + geiss, dedicated to the sherwin maxawow.
It runs on this preview's own WebGL2 renderer, and
visualizer/src/studies/dive.ts therefore contains GLSL ported from that
preset: its warp and comp shader bodies, and its frame and pixel equations
rewritten as shader code. That file is covered by the MIT licence reproduced
below, and carries the same attribution in its header. The new motion
controls and song score belong to this preview. The original preset authors
retain credit.
