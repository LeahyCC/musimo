Musimo visualizer preview

Butterchurn 3.0.0-beta.5 and butterchurn-presets 2.4.7
https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn
https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn-presets

The preview retains these preset authors:
martin - witchcraft reloaded
Flexi, martin + geiss - dedicated to the sherwin maxawow

The Dive study adapts the Sherwin Maxawow preset. It now runs on this preview's
own WebGL2 renderer, and visualizer/src/studies/dive.ts therefore contains GLSL
ported from that preset: its warp and comp shader bodies, and its frame and
pixel equations rewritten as shader code. That file is covered by the MIT
licence reproduced below, and carries the same attribution in its header. The
new motion controls and song score belong to this preview. The original preset
authors retain credit.
